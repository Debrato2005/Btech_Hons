Log-Mel Spectrum. Two 1D CNN layers 
compress the sequence before it 
enters the Transformer Encoder.

Learned Positional Encoding: Trainable 
vectors added to each audio frame so 
the Transformer understands the order 
of the speech sequence.
•
The Decoder generates one text token 
at a time using cross-attention over 
Encoder output.
•
Special tokens at the start of the Decoder tell Whisper which 
task to perform, such as Speech Transcription, Speech 
Translation, Language Identification & Voice Activity Detection.
