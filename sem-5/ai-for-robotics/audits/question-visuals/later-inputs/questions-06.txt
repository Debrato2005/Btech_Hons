•
Robot navigation is a designed process 
toward a target position while avoiding 
obstacles. 
•
All navigation systems, whether 
classical or AI-based, follow these four 
fundamental stages:
– Perception: the robot uses its sensors 
to extract meaningful information.
– Localization: the robot determines its 
location in the working space.
Robot Navigation Pipeline
– Cognition and path planning: the robot decides how to 
steer to achieve its goal.
– Motion control: the robot regulates its motion to 
accomplish the desired trajectory.
