Hydraulic Actuators : These utilize hydraulic cylinders or 
vane actuators to produce high forces in compact packages.
•
Hydraulic actuators provide a very high power-to-weight 
ratio and can directly drive joints without reduction systems.
•
But they require bulky peripheral equipment and suffer
from fluid leaks and seal friction, making accurate force
control difficult.

•
Pneumatic Actuators : These are similar to hydraulics but 
use compressed air.
•
They are cleaner than hydraulics (air seeps out rather than 
fluid), but they are notoriously difficult to control accurately 
due to the compressibility of air and high seal friction.
