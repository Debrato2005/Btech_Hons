•
FER enables robots to estimate a user's emotional 
state from facial expressions captured by a camera. 
•
Rather than training a CNN from scratch, modern 
FER systems use transfer learning by fine-tuning 
pre-trained Deep CNN models on labelled facial 
expression datasets. 
•
The resulting model classifies facial expressions into 
discrete emotion categories such as happiness, 
sadness, anger, fear and surprise. 
Facial Emotion Recognition (FER)
•
One of the most widely 
used perception modules 
in social robots.

– Feature Extraction: Train only the classifier.

• Full fine-tuning generally

•
Human emotions are reflected not only in what is 
said, but also in how it is spoken.
•
Speech features such as pitch, speaking rate, energy, 
and MFCCs provide important emotional cues.
•
Deep Learning models learn these patterns to 
estimate the speaker's emotional state.
•
SER complements language understanding and 
improves emotion-aware Human-Robot Interaction.
Speech Emotion Recognition (SER)
•
Future emotion-aware robots combine text 
understanding with Speech Emotion Recognition (SER) 
and Facial Emotion Recognition (FER) to obtain a more 
reliable estimate of the user's emotional state.
