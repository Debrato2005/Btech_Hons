What is Odometry?
– Odometry is the process of estimating the 
robot’s position and orientation using wheel 
encoder measurements.
Wheel Encoder Odometry
•
Quadrature encoders measure wheel rotation. 
•
Encoder counts are converted into wheel displacement. 
•
Robot pose is estimated using differential-drive 
kinematics. 
•
Odometry provides continuous position estimation. 
•
Errors accumulate over time due to wheel slip and 
uneven terrain.
