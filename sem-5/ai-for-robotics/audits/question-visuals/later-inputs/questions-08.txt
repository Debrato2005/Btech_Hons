Path planning assumes that robot already 
knows both environment map and its own pose. 
•
In practice, these are obtained through 
perception, localization, and mapping.
•
Perception extracts information about the environment 
from sensor measurements (LiDAR, cameras, and time-of-flight (ToF) sensors). 
•
Localization estimates robot's pose with respect to a known map or reference 
frame, while mapping constructs a spatial representation 
of the environment when no prior map exists. 
•
Together, these processes provide the information needed 
for autonomous navigation, path planning, & motion control.
