The Problem: Traditional speech recognition systems 
rely on a complex pipeline of many different stages 
to process audio and translate it to text
– The Data Challenge: Speech data is inherently 
mismatched with text, as audio features are extracted 
every 10 ms, the acoustic sequence is 5 to 19 times 
longer than the resulting text sequence.
Limitations of Traditional Systems
•
The Solution: Modern sequence-to-sequence Deep 
Learning models (like CNNs, Transformers) are needed 
because they can elegantly compress this massive 
sequence mismatch.
─End-to-End Efficiency: A single DL model can learn to map 
the audio directly to text, completely replacing the multi-
stage traditional pipeline.
