Definition
– An LDR (Light Dependent Resistor) is a sensor whose resistance changes with 
light intensity.
•
Working Principle
– Bright light → low resistance
– Low light → high resistance
– Used in voltage divider circuit
– Arduino reads voltage change
•
Applications in Robotics
– Line-following robots
– Automatic street-light robots
– Solar tracking robots
– Obstacle detection (light-based)
