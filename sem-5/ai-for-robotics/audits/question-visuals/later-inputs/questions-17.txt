Definition
– The DHT11 is a low-cost digital sensor used to measure ambient temperature 
and relative humidity.
•
Working Principle
– Uses a thermistor to measure temperature
– Uses a capacitive humidity sensor to detect moisture
– Internal ADC converts data into digital format
– Data is sent using a single-wire serial communication
•
Applications in Robotics
– Environment monitoring robots
– Smart agriculture robots
– Weather stations
– Fire-safety and HVAC robots
