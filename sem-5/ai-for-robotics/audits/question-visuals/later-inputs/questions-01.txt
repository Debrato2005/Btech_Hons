2. Uniform-Cost Search (UCS)
•
Mathematical Notation: Minimizes f(v)=g(v), 
where g(v) is the cumulative cost (distance) from the 
start to the current vertex v.
•
Data Structures: It uses a priority queue ordered 
from smallest to largest value of g(v).
